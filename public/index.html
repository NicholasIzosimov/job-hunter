    Snippets

    Reusable text for applications. {{company}}, {{title}}, {{location}} and {{date}} come from the job; {{name}}, {{phone}}, {{email}}, {{linkedin}} and {{portfolio}} from your Profile. Which letter goes with which kind of job is set in Profile & autofill; the default snippet is the fallback.

    The Fill button

    1. Show your bookmarks bar: Ctrl+Shift+B.
    2. Drag this button onto it: ⚡ Fill application
    3. Click Apply on a job here, then click ⚡ Fill application on the job's form.

    It fills empty fields with the details below, pastes the right cover letter and attaches the right CV, then shows what it did. It never submits: you check and press Submit. After changing anything below, drag the button again (the old one keeps the old details).

    Chrome may ask once per job site to "access devices on your local network": allow it so the button can fetch your CV and letter. Greenhouse and Ashby sites block this anyway; there the button fills the text fields, the letter is already on your clipboard (Ctrl+V), and you attach the CV yourself.

    Mail from companies

    Job Hunter reads your Gmail inbox and shows emails from companies you've applied to, on the matching job. It only reads: it never marks anything as read, sends, moves or deletes. Everything stays on this computer.

    Gmail connection
